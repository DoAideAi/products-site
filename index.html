Before you start

A few practical questions.

Who is DoAide GST for?

Small accounting firms, chartered accountants and finance teams that review invoices and prepare GST returns for Indian businesses.

Does DoAide file returns automatically?

DoAide helps prepare and export return data. You or your accountant must review the figures and complete submission on the GST portal. Preparing a return does not file it.

Can I try it before arranging a demo?

Yes. The free GST tools are available without signing in. Open the GST app to explore account features, or check its pricing page for current plans and limits.

What should I bring to a demo?

Tell us how you receive invoices, your approximate client volume and what slows down reconciliation. Use anonymised sample data when discussing your workflow; no live client documents are needed for the initial request.
