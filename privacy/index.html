Privacy and cookies

Updated 3 November 2026. DoAide is operated by Apprend Technologies.

Demo requests

When you request a demo, we store your name, firm, reply email, approximate client count and optional notes in our private support inbox. We use them to respond and arrange your demo. A request is not an appointment until a time is agreed.

Our form also records a browser description and IP address to limit abuse. IP addresses in the inbox are cleared after 30 days. Please do not include client invoices, GSTINs or financial records in demo requests.

Site analytics

We use Umami to understand visits and product events such as completed signups, pilot starts and successful uploads. Demo form contents are not sent to analytics.

Optional advertising measurement

If you choose Allow ad measurement, Google Ads uses cookies to connect visits from ads with completed demo requests, signups, trial starts, product use and verified purchases. These events do not include your name, email, GSTIN or invoice contents. Purchase events may include the subscription payment amount and an order reference. Google processes this information under its privacy policy.

Your choice applies to doaide.com and gst.doaide.com for six months. Choosing Decline does not affect the service. You can change your choice using the Ad measurement button on either site.

Contact and deletion requests

Email support@doaide.com to ask about your information or request removal of a demo request.
